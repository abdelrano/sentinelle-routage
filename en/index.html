Sentinelle Routage has closed.

Service discontinued on 14 October 2026.

Thank you to the roughly 700 operators who checked the routing health of their ASN, and to the early partners who trialled the monitoring.

Nothing has been or will be charged to you. Subscriptions are cancelled, alerts have stopped and the monitoring data has been deleted.

To keep monitoring your ASN and ROAs for free:

  • bgp.tools: alerts on your own ASN after verification
  • BGPalerter: open-source, self-hosted BGP and RPKI monitoring
  • RIPEstat: RPKI status, visibility and history of your prefixes

The August 2026 RPKI barometer remains available.
